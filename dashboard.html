Straight-line trim (alpha)

The slower side always runs at the chosen speed. The faster side runs at speed × alpha so the robot drives straight. Saved on the laptop. To calibrate: drive forward at Turbo; if it pulls to one side, the OTHER side is the faster one. Lower alpha until it goes straight.
